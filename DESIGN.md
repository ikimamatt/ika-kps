## Brand & Style
The design system embodies the heritage, prestige, and vibrant camaraderie of Ikatan Keluarga Alumni Sekolah Nasional KPS Balikpapan (IKA KPS). Grounded in the proud identity of Balikpapan as "Kota Minyak" and the academic rigor of Sekolah KPS, the aesthetic balances institutional gravitas with youthful almamater energy.

The visual style blends **Corporate Modernism** with **Maritime/Petroleum Academic Prestige**:
- **Tone:** Authoritative yet welcoming, established, connected, and forward-looking.
- **Audience:** Cross-generational alumni across industries and geographies, prospective partners, educators, and current students.
- **Visual Expression:** Razor-clean white and cool tinted surfaces framed by deep marine navy structural lines, energized by bright Balikpapan cyan accents, and punctuated with high-impact flame red call-to-actions.

## Colors
The palette directly extracts and codifies the official heraldic emblem of IKA KPS:
- **Primary Navy (`#092B5A`):** The foundational pillar representing institutional stability, trust, depth, and the iconic oil derrick structure. Used for major surfaces, headers, high-emphasis text, and core borders.
- **Secondary Cyan (`#00A3E0`):** Drawn from the roundel core. Conveys optimism, youthful connection, clarity, and regional pride along the coastal energy hub. Applied to active states, badges, link highlights, and secondary actions.
- **Tertiary Flame Red (`#D92525`):** Derived from the burning torch crowning the derrick. Represents the flame of continuous knowledge (*semangat membara*) and passion. Reserved strictly for critical calls to action, active alerts, urgent announcements, and celebratory achievements.
- **Neutral Foundation (`#FFFFFF` & `#F4F8FC`):** Pure white combined with an ultra-clean cool ice tint to provide high contrast, uncluttered layouts, and crisp readability. Slate neutrals (`#4A5568`, `#1A202C`) provide intermediate typographical balance.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans delivers a geometric, precise, and approachable typographic architecture. Headlines leverage bold and extra-bold weights with negative tracking to convey institutional strength similar to academic crests. Body text uses regular and medium weights with balanced line heights to maximize legibility across alumni directories, event schedules, and thought leadership articles.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to an 8-point geometric scale driven by structural harmony:
- **Grid Structure:** A 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max container 1240px) with 24px gutters, transitioning to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile devices with 16px margins.
- **Rhythm:** Dense yet breathable rhythm. Data tables and administrative directory lists use tight spacing (`space-xs` to `space-sm`), while promotional banners, portal cards, and hero sections utilize expanded padding (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to establish hierarchy.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth in the design system uses crisp structural layering rather than heavy ambient blur:
- **Tonal Layering:** The primary canvas rests on `#FFFFFF` and `#F4F8FC`. Elevated cards sit on crisp white with a faint structural hairline border (`1px solid rgba(9, 43, 90, 0.08)`).
- **Subtle Oceanic Shadows:** Elevated components (modals, dropdowns, sticky action bars) cast subtle navy-tinted ambient shadows (`0 8px 24px -4px rgba(9, 43, 90, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(9, 43, 90, 0.04)`).
- **Flame Focus Ring:** Interactive states employ a 2px offset cyan focus halo (`rgba(0, 163, 224, 0.4)`) to maintain accessibility and prominent tactile feedback.

## Shapes
A roundedness level of `2` provides modern, polished geometry that softens the corporate tone without feeling casual. Standard elements (buttons, inputs, notification badges) feature `0.5rem` (8px) corner radii. Larger container cards and modal sheets use `1rem` (16px), while full circular pills are reserved for status tags, batch badges, and user profile avatars.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid Deep Navy (`#092B5A`) background with crisp white typography. Hover: `#061D3D`. Focus: 3px Cyan glow.
- **Accent / CTA:** Flame Red (`#D92525`) background with white typography for donation campaigns, urgent RSVP, and registration actions. Hover: `#B81B1B`.
- **Secondary:** Transparent fill with 1.5px solid Deep Navy or Cyan border, paired with corresponding text color.
- **Subtle / Ghost:** Soft Cyan tint (`rgba(0, 163, 224, 0.08)`) with `#00A3E0` text.

### Chips & Badges
- **Alumni Batch Badge:** Pill-shaped, subtle cool-blue surface (`#E6F4FB`), text `#092B5A`, border `1px solid rgba(0, 163, 224, 0.25)`.
- **Status Badges:** Active/Verified in Cyan/Navy; Urgent/Action Required in soft Red tint (`#FDECEC`) with `#D92525` text.

### Input Fields
- White fill, 1px border (`#CFDDEB`), rounded at 8px. Text typed in `#092B5A`.
- Active focus state transitions border to `#00A3E0` with a 3px soft cyan ring (`rgba(0, 163, 224, 0.15)`).

### Cards
- Pure white surface enclosed with `1px solid rgba(9, 43, 90, 0.06)`, radius `16px`. Header strips or accent badges carry the signature Navy-to-Cyan gradient or solid Navy emblem accents.

### Lists & Directories
- Clean zebra or separated-row layouts with dividing lines colored `#E8EFF7`. High-density contact data for alumni networks with prominent graduation year badges and verified affiliation checkmarks.
